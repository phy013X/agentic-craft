## Superpowers (installed on demand, not vendored)

This repository does **not** bundle Superpowers source. When you run the initializer with Superpowers enabled (the default), it fetches [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers) at a pinned tag and, to satisfy the MIT attribution requirement, copies its license into your project at `.claude/SUPERPOWERS_LICENSE` and records the version at `.claude/SUPERPOWERS_VERSION`.
